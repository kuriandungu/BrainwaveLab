Before you listen (setup checklist)
  • Use wired headphones or Bluetooth earbuds for binaural mode — you need real left/right separation between your ears.
  • If your phone or earbuds have a spatial-audio or sound-enhancement/EQ feature, turn it off — these can mix left and right and destroy binaural beats.
  • Isochronic mode works fine on the phone's built-in speaker — no headphones needed.
  • Keep volume comfortable-low; entrainment does not need loudness.
  • Not for use while driving; stop if you feel discomfort. Not a medical device.
